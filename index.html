The Process

Simple from start to finish

We've streamlined every step so you can focus on what matters — your students. From choosing a look to receiving your finished composites, we handle it all.

1

Choose your package

Select the package that fits your class size, from small groups of 5 to large school-wide orders of 15 or more students.

2

Pick a template

Browse our professionally designed layouts — classic grid, panel, mosaic, and more — or upload your own custom composite to match.

3

Submit your details

Upload your school logo, choose your preferred font, and provide class information. We handle the design work from there.

4

Approve & receive

We'll send you a digital proof for review. Once approved, your composites are printed and shipped directly to your school.
